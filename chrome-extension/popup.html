Web CompanionChrome edition · v2.3
HealthShared across tabs
ExperienceLevel 1

HP decreases after 15 seconds of uninterrupted mascot idle time in the active tab, then loses 1 HP every 3 seconds. Ongoing mouse/keyboard activity recovers 1 HP every 3 seconds after a 5-second activity streak. At 0 HP the mascot becomes KO and requires Restore health. EXP is reserved for a future reward system. Drag the mascot to move it. Right-click it for more actions. It appears on ordinary websites only.

Not showing on a site? Open chrome://extensions → Details → Site access → On all sites, then refresh. Protected Chrome pages remain unsupported.
